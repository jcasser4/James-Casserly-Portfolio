Process

From test requirement to production-ready fixture.

01

Define the test

Clarify validation goal, pass/fail criteria, operator constraints, safety requirements, and measurement needs.

02

Concept + CAD

Develop fast layouts, mechanisms, enclosure concepts, ergonomics, and service paths in CAD.

03

Fabricate + integrate

Build with extrusion, machined parts, 3D prints, wiring, pneumatic components, sensors, and fixtures.

04

Validate + handoff

Debug the tester, refine the workflow, document the design, and support technicians or vendors.
